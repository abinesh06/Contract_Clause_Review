MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2025
(the "Effective Date") by and between Apex Digital Solutions, Inc., a Delaware
corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Harbor Street, Wilmington,
Delaware ("Provider"), and Northwind Retail Group, LLC, a Michigan limited liability
company with its principal place of business at 1120 Lakeshore Drive, Grand Rapids,
Michigan ("Client"). Provider and Client are each referred to as a "Party" and
collectively as the "Parties."

1. SERVICES

1.1 Provider shall perform the professional services described in one or more
statements of work executed by the Parties and referencing this Agreement (each, a
"Statement of Work" or "SOW"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope of
services, deliverables, milestones, fees, and any assumptions or dependencies
applicable to the engagement (the "Services").

1.2 In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and any Statement of Work, the
terms of this Agreement shall control unless the Statement of Work expressly states
that it supersedes a specific provision of this Agreement and is signed by an
authorized representative of each Party.

1.3 Provider may perform the Services through its employees, contractors, or
affiliates, provided that Provider remains responsible for the performance of the
Services in accordance with this Agreement.

2. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 Client shall provide Provider with timely access to such personnel, systems,
facilities, data, and information as Provider reasonably requires to perform the
Services. Provider shall not be responsible for any delay or deficiency in the
Services to the extent caused by Client's failure to provide such access.

2.2 Client shall designate a project sponsor authorized to make decisions, approve
deliverables, and resolve escalations on Client's behalf.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial
term of twelve (12) months (the "Initial Term").

3.2 Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for
successive one-year terms unless Client delivers written notice of non-renewal to
Provider no later than thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current Term.
Each renewal term shall be subject to Provider's then-current standard rates, which
Provider may adjust in its discretion upon notice posted to its customer portal.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Client shall pay the fees set forth in each Statement of Work. Unless otherwise
stated, invoices are issued monthly in arrears and are due within thirty (30) days of
the invoice date.

4.2 Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and
one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law,
whichever is lower.

4.3 Client shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, pre-approved travel and
out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with the Services.

4.4 All fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added, and similar taxes, which shall
be Client's responsibility other than taxes on Provider's net income.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 Each Party may disclose to the other certain non-public information relating to its
business, technology, customers, or operations ("Confidential Information"). The
receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely to perform its obligations
under this Agreement and shall protect it using at least the degree of care it applies
to its own confidential information of similar importance, and in no event less than
reasonable care.

5.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly
available through no fault of the receiving Party, was rightfully known to the
receiving Party without restriction prior to disclosure, or is independently developed
without reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

5.3 The obligations in this Section 5 shall survive for three (3) years following
expiration or termination of this Agreement.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 Upon Provider's receipt of full payment for the applicable Services, Client shall
own the deliverables specifically identified as work product in the applicable
Statement of Work.

6.2 Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing materials,
methodologies, tools, templates, and know-how, including any improvements thereto, and
grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use such materials solely as
incorporated into the deliverables.

7. WARRANTIES

7.1 Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner by personnel with suitable skill and experience.

7.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION 7, THE SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES ARE
PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED,
OR STATUTORY, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A
PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

8. TERMINATION

8.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work upon thirty (30)
days written notice to the other Party of a material breach if such breach remains
uncured at the end of that period.

8.2 Upon termination, Client shall pay Provider for all Services performed and expenses
incurred through the effective date of termination.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 Provider's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement,
whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client
under the applicable Statement of Work during the three (3) months preceding the event
giving rise to the claim.

9.2 Client's liability under this Agreement shall be unlimited. Client acknowledges and
agrees that Client shall be liable for any and all damages arising from its use of the
deliverables, its breach of this Agreement, or its instructions to Provider, without
limitation as to amount, and Client expressly waives any limitation of liability that
might otherwise apply to Client under this Agreement or at law.

10. INDEMNIFICATION

10.1 Client shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Provider, its affiliates, and
their respective officers, directors, employees, contractors, and agents from and
against any and all claims of any kind whatsoever, and any and all liabilities, losses,
damages, fines, penalties, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees)
arising out of or in any way related to this Agreement, the Services, or the
deliverables, regardless of the cause and without regard to fault, including claims
arising from the negligence of Provider or its personnel.

10.2 Provider shall have no indemnification obligation to Client under this Agreement.

11. INSURANCE

11.1 Each Party shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not
less than one million dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence and shall provide a
certificate of insurance upon request.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to
its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 The Parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through discussion
between senior representatives before initiating litigation. Any action shall be brought
exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each
Party consents to the jurisdiction of those courts.

13. NOTICES

13.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, nationally
recognized overnight courier, or certified mail to the addresses stated above, and are
effective upon receipt.

14. GENERAL

14.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written
consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all
of its assets.

14.2 Neither Party shall be liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable
control, including natural disasters, labor disputes, or failures of public
infrastructure.

14.3 The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a
partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

14.4 This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work, constitutes the entire
agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior
proposals and discussions. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.5 If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions
shall remain in full force and effect.
