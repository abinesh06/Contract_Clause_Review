CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
February 10, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Bramble & Hoyt Advisory LLC, a
Massachusetts limited liability company with offices at 15 Beacon Court, Boston,
Massachusetts ("Advisor"), and Fairfield Instruments Corporation, a New York
corporation with offices at 700 Canal Street, Rochester, New York ("Client"). Advisor
and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. SERVICES

1.1 Advisor shall provide the operational advisory services described in Schedule 1
(the "Services"). The Parties may add or modify Services only by written amendment
signed by both Parties.

1.2 Advisor shall perform the Services with the degree of skill and care ordinarily
exercised by qualified professionals performing comparable services.

1.3 Advisor shall keep Client reasonably informed of progress and shall provide a
written status summary at the end of each month during the Term.

2. TERM

2.1 The Term begins on the Effective Date and ends twelve (12) months later.

2.2 This Agreement expires at the end of the Term. Any continuation of the Services
requires a written amendment signed by both Parties before the Term ends.

3. FEES, EXPENSES, AND PAYMENT

3.1 Client shall pay Advisor the fees stated in Schedule 1.

3.2 Advisor shall submit invoices monthly. Client shall pay undisputed amounts within
thirty (30) days after receiving an invoice.

3.3 Client shall reimburse reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Client
approved in writing in advance, supported by receipts.

3.4 Each Party is responsible for its own taxes arising from this Agreement, except that
Client is responsible for any applicable sales or use tax on the Services.

4. CLIENT COOPERATION

4.1 Client shall make available the personnel, records, and information that Advisor
reasonably needs to perform the Services, and shall designate a primary contact
authorized to provide direction on Client's behalf.

4.2 If Client's delay in providing cooperation affects the schedule, the Parties shall
agree in writing on a revised schedule, and neither Party shall be treated as in breach
solely because of that delay.

5. NON-EXCLUSIVE RELATIONSHIP

5.1 This Agreement is non-exclusive. Advisor may provide services to other clients,
including others in Client's industry, subject to Advisor's confidentiality obligations
under Section 7.

5.2 Client may obtain services of the same or a similar nature from any other party at
any time, and from its own personnel, without restriction and without notice to Advisor.

6. PERSONNEL

6.1 Advisor's personnel are employees or contractors of Advisor and are not employees of
Client. Advisor is responsible for their compensation, taxes, and benefits.

6.2 During the Term and for twelve (12) months afterward, neither Party shall knowingly
solicit for employment any individual of the other Party who was directly involved in
the Services, except that general advertisements not targeted at such individuals are
permitted.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each Party shall protect the other Party's non-public business, technical, and
financial information ("Confidential Information") using at least reasonable care, and
shall use it only to perform or receive the Services.

7.2 Confidential Information excludes information that is publicly available through no
fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without a duty of
confidence before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the disclosing
Party's Confidential Information, or is rightfully received from a third party without
restriction.

7.3 A receiving Party compelled by law to disclose Confidential Information shall, where
legally permitted, notify the disclosing Party in advance and disclose only what is
required.

7.4 These obligations continue for three (3) years after the Term ends. Upon request
after termination or expiration, each Party shall return or destroy the other's
Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or as required
by law or professional record-keeping rules.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Client owns the written reports and recommendations that Advisor prepares
specifically for Client under this Agreement, effective upon payment of the fees for the
Services that produced them.

8.2 Advisor retains ownership of the frameworks, templates, and general knowledge and
experience it uses to perform the Services, and grants Client a perpetual,
non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use those materials to the extent they are
included in the reports described in Section 8.1.

8.3 Nothing in this Agreement transfers either Party's trademarks or requires either
Party to use the other's name in publicity without prior written consent.

9. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

9.1 Each Party represents that it is duly organized and in good standing, that it has
the authority to enter into this Agreement, and that this Agreement does not conflict
with any of its other obligations.

9.2 Advisor warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner. If Client notifies Advisor of a breach of this warranty within
thirty (30) days of the affected Services, Advisor shall re-perform them at no
additional charge.

9.3 Advisor does not guarantee any particular business outcome or financial result, and
Client remains responsible for its own business decisions.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or
consequential damages, or for lost profits or lost business opportunity, arising out of
or relating to this Agreement.

10.2 Each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not
exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months
before the event giving rise to the claim.

10.3 Section 10.1 and Section 10.2 do not limit either Party's liability for its breach
of Section 7, for fraud, or for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10.4 The Parties agree that the allocation of risk in this Section 10 is a material part
of the bargain and is reflected in the fees.

11. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Advisor shall defend Client against third-party claims that the reports described
in Section 8.1 infringe a United States copyright or trade secret, and shall pay
settlements it approves and damages finally awarded on such claims.

11.2 Client shall defend Advisor against third-party claims arising from Client's
implementation of Advisor's recommendations in a manner Advisor advised against in
writing, and shall pay settlements it approves and damages finally awarded on such
claims.

11.3 Each indemnity in this Section 11 is conditioned on the indemnified Party giving
prompt written notice of the claim, permitting the indemnifying Party to control the
defense, and cooperating at the indemnifying Party's reasonable expense. Neither Party
shall settle a claim in a way that imposes a non-monetary obligation on the other
without that Party's written consent.

11.4 Neither Party is obligated to indemnify the other for claims caused by the
indemnified Party's own negligence or misconduct.

12. TERMINATION

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party
ninety (90) days prior written notice.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches it
and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice that
describes the breach in reasonable detail.

12.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party becomes
insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed
over a substantial part of its assets.

12.4 Upon expiration or termination, Client shall pay for Services performed through the
effective date, Advisor shall deliver work in progress that Client has paid for, and
Sections 7, 8, 10, 11, and 14 shall survive.

13. INSURANCE

13.1 Each Party shall maintain the insurance coverage customary for its business,
including commercial general liability coverage of at least one million dollars
($1,000,000) per occurrence, and shall provide a certificate of insurance on reasonable
request.

14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, excluding its
conflict of laws rules.

14.2 Before starting litigation, the Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute
through good-faith discussions between senior representatives for at least thirty (30)
days after written notice of the dispute.

14.3 Any litigation shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in New York
County, New York, and each Party consents to venue there.

15. GENERAL

15.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement or delegate its obligations without the
other Party's prior written consent, except to a successor by merger or by purchase of
substantially all of its assets, provided the successor assumes this Agreement in
writing.

15.2 Neither Party is liable for a failure or delay in performance caused by an event
beyond its reasonable control, provided that the affected Party notifies the other
promptly, mitigates where practicable, and resumes performance as soon as it reasonably
can. If such an event continues for more than sixty (60) days, either Party may
terminate this Agreement on written notice.

15.3 The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create a
partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship, and neither Party may
bind the other.

15.4 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized
overnight courier, or by certified mail to the addresses stated above, and are effective
on receipt.

15.5 A waiver of any provision is effective only if in writing and signed by the waiving
Party, and does not waive any other provision or any later breach.

15.6 This Agreement, including Schedule 1, is the complete and exclusive statement of
the Parties' agreement on this subject and supersedes all prior proposals and
understandings. It may be amended only by a written document signed by both Parties.

15.7 If a court finds any provision unenforceable, that provision shall be limited to
the minimum extent necessary so that the remainder of this Agreement continues in
effect.

15.8 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature,
each of which is an original.
