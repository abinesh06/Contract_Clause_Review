PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made effective as of
June 17, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Cedarline Analytics LLC, an Oregon limited
liability company located at 88 Willamette Avenue, Portland, Oregon ("Consultant"), and
Harbor Point Medical Group, P.C., a Washington professional corporation located at
2400 Pier Road, Seattle, Washington ("Company"). Consultant and Company are each a
"Party" and together the "Parties."

1. ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE

1.1 Company engages Consultant to provide data analytics and reporting services as
described in Exhibit A (the "Services"). Additional services may be added by a written
change order signed by both Parties.

1.2 Consultant shall perform the Services in accordance with the schedule set out in
Exhibit A and shall promptly notify Company of any circumstance that Consultant
reasonably believes will delay a milestone.

1.3 Consultant shall assign a lead analyst to the engagement and shall not replace that
individual without first notifying Company and proposing a comparably qualified
replacement.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twenty-four (24)
months (the "Term").

2.2 The Term may be extended only by a written amendment signed by both Parties. This
Agreement does not renew automatically, and neither Party is obligated to extend it.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Company shall pay Consultant the fees stated in Exhibit A. Consultant shall invoice
monthly, and Company shall pay undisputed invoice amounts within thirty (30) days of
receipt.

3.2 If Company disputes any portion of an invoice in good faith, Company shall pay the
undisputed portion when due and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the
disputed portion within thirty (30) days.

3.3 Fees are firm for the first twelve (12) months of the Term. Thereafter, Consultant
may propose a rate adjustment of not more than three percent (3%) per year, effective
only upon Company's written acceptance.

4. COMPANY OBLIGATIONS

4.1 Company shall provide Consultant with the data, systems access, and subject-matter
contacts reasonably necessary for the Services, and shall be responsible for the
accuracy and completeness of data it supplies.

4.2 Company shall review deliverables and provide written acceptance or a description of
deficiencies within ten (10) business days of delivery. Deliverables not rejected within
that period are deemed accepted.

5. PREFERRED SUPPLIER ARRANGEMENT

5.1 During the Term, Company shall not engage any third party to perform analytics
services substantially similar to the Services with respect to the clinical datasets
identified in Exhibit A, without Consultant's prior written consent, which shall not be
unreasonably withheld or delayed.

5.2 For the avoidance of doubt, Section 5.1 does not restrict Company from performing
such services using its own internal personnel, from engaging third parties for services
outside the scope described in Exhibit A, or from engaging any third party after the
Term ends.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

6.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the non-public information of the other Party
that is disclosed in connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information"), shall
use it only as needed to perform this Agreement, and shall protect it with at least
reasonable care.

6.2 The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its employees and
professional advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality
obligations no less protective than those in this Section 6.

6.3 If the receiving Party is compelled by law to disclose Confidential Information, it
shall give the disclosing Party prompt notice, to the extent legally permitted, so the
disclosing Party may seek a protective order.

6.4 These obligations continue for five (5) years after the end of the Term, and
indefinitely with respect to protected health information.

7. DATA PROTECTION

7.1 To the extent Consultant handles protected health information on Company's behalf,
the Parties shall execute a business associate agreement in the form attached as
Exhibit B, which is incorporated by reference.

7.2 Consultant shall maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards
appropriate to the sensitivity of the data it processes, and shall notify Company
without unreasonable delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after
confirming a security incident affecting Company data.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 Company owns the reports, dashboards, and analyses delivered under this Agreement
upon payment of the associated fees.

8.2 Consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, scripts, and general
methodologies, and grants Company a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use them as
embedded in the deliverables.

8.3 Each Party retains ownership of its trademarks and may not use the other's
trademarks without prior written consent.

9. WARRANTIES

9.1 Each Party represents that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement and
that doing so does not conflict with any other obligation.

9.2 Consultant warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and
workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry practice. Company's
remedy for a breach of this warranty is re-performance of the deficient Services or,
if re-performance is not commercially practicable, a refund of the fees paid for the
deficient Services.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential
damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

10.2 Each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not
exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months
preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

10.3 The limitations in this Section 10 do not apply to a Party's breach of its
confidentiality obligations, its indemnification obligations under Section 11, or its
gross negligence or willful misconduct.

11. INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Consultant shall defend Company against third-party claims alleging that the
deliverables infringe a United States patent, copyright, or trade secret, and shall pay
damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claims.

11.2 Company shall defend Consultant against third-party claims arising from data that
Company supplied to Consultant in breach of Company's representations in Section 4.1,
and shall pay damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement of such claims.

11.3 The indemnified Party shall give prompt notice of any claim, allow the indemnifying
Party to control the defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying
Party's expense. No settlement that imposes an obligation on the indemnified Party is
binding without its consent.

12. TERMINATION

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days
prior written notice to the other Party.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches
it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice
describing the breach.

12.3 Upon termination, Company shall pay for Services performed through the effective
date of termination, and each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential
Information upon request.

13. INSURANCE

13.1 Consultant shall maintain professional liability insurance of at least two million
dollars ($2,000,000) per claim and commercial general liability insurance of at least
one million dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence, and shall provide certificates of
insurance on request.

14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, excluding its
conflict of laws rules.

14.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation
between senior representatives, and then through non-binding mediation in Seattle,
Washington, before commencing litigation.

15. GENERAL

15.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent,
except to a successor by merger or acquisition of substantially all of its assets.

15.2 Consultant is an independent contractor. Neither Party is the agent of the other,
and neither may bind the other.

15.3 Neither Party is liable for failures to perform caused by events beyond its
reasonable control, provided the affected Party gives prompt notice and resumes
performance as soon as practicable.

15.4 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above by hand delivery,
overnight courier, or certified mail.

15.5 This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, is the entire agreement of the Parties
on this subject and replaces all prior discussions. Amendments must be in writing and
signed by both Parties.

15.6 If any provision is found unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent
necessary to make it enforceable, and the remainder of the Agreement shall continue in
effect.
