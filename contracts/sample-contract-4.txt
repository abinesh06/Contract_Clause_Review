SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software Subscription Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of
October 6, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between Vertex Cloud Systems, Inc., a
Colorado corporation with offices at 2200 Front Range Parkway, Denver, Colorado
("Vendor"), and Lakeside Freight Partners, LLC, an Illinois limited liability company
with offices at 350 Dock Street, Chicago, Illinois ("Customer"). Vendor and Customer are
each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. SUBSCRIPTION AND LICENSE

1.1 Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the
hosted software platform described in Schedule A (the "Platform") during the Term, solely
for Customer's internal business operations.

1.2 Customer may permit its employees and contractors to use the Platform, provided that
Customer remains responsible for their compliance with this Agreement.

1.3 Customer shall not reverse engineer the Platform, resell access to it, or use it to
build a competing product.

1.4 Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in the Platform, including all
improvements and modifications to it.

2. IMPLEMENTATION AND SUPPORT

2.1 Vendor shall provide the implementation assistance described in Schedule A and shall
assign an implementation lead for the first ninety (90) days following the Effective
Date.

2.2 Vendor shall provide technical support during business hours and shall use
commercially reasonable efforts to respond to support requests within the target
response times stated in Schedule B.

2.3 Vendor shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days notice before making a change
to the Platform that materially reduces its core functionality.

3. TERM

3.1 The Term begins on the Effective Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months.

3.2 This Agreement expires at the end of the Term. Continuation of the subscription
requires a written amendment signed by both Parties before the Term ends, and neither
Party is obligated to agree to one.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 Customer shall pay the subscription fees stated in Schedule A, invoiced annually in
advance.

4.2 Customer shall pay undisputed invoice amounts within thirty (30) days after receiving
an invoice. If Customer disputes an amount in good faith, Customer shall pay the
undisputed portion when due and the Parties shall work together to resolve the remainder
within thirty (30) days.

4.3 Subscription fees are fixed for the first twenty-four (24) months. Any later
adjustment takes effect only upon Customer's written acceptance.

4.4 Each Party is responsible for its own taxes, except that Customer is responsible for
applicable sales and use taxes on the subscription.

5. CUSTOMER DATA

5.1 Customer owns all data it or its users submit to the Platform ("Customer Data").
Vendor may use Customer Data only to provide and support the Platform.

5.2 Vendor shall maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate
to the sensitivity of Customer Data, and shall notify Customer without unreasonable
delay after confirming a security incident affecting Customer Data.

5.3 During the Term and for sixty (60) days afterward, Customer may export Customer Data
in a documented machine-readable format at no additional charge.

6. NON-EXCLUSIVE RELATIONSHIP

6.1 This Agreement is non-exclusive. Customer may license, purchase, build, or operate
software of the same or a similar nature from or with any other party at any time,
without restriction and without notice to Vendor.

6.2 Vendor may license the Platform to any other party, including others in Customer's
industry, subject to Vendor's confidentiality obligations under Section 8.

7. SERVICE LEVELS

7.1 Vendor shall make the Platform available at least 99.5% of the time each calendar
month, excluding scheduled maintenance for which Vendor gave at least seventy-two (72)
hours notice.

7.2 If Vendor fails to meet the availability commitment in a given month, Customer is
entitled to the service credits stated in Schedule B.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each Party shall protect the other Party's non-public business, technical, and
financial information ("Confidential Information") using at least reasonable care, and
shall use it only to perform or receive under this Agreement.

8.2 Confidential Information excludes information that is publicly available through no
fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without a duty of
confidence before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the disclosing
Party's Confidential Information, or is rightfully received from a third party without
restriction.

8.3 A receiving Party compelled by law to disclose Confidential Information shall, where
legally permitted, notify the disclosing Party in advance and disclose only what is
required.

8.4 These obligations continue for three (3) years after the Term ends.

9. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

9.1 Each Party represents that it is duly organized and in good standing and has the
authority to enter into this Agreement.

9.2 Vendor warrants that the Platform will perform materially in accordance with the
documentation in Schedule A. If it does not, and Customer notifies Vendor within thirty
(30) days of discovering the problem, Vendor shall correct it at no additional charge.

9.3 Vendor does not warrant that the Platform will be error-free or uninterrupted.

10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

10.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or
consequential damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

10.2 Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall
not exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the twelve (12)
months before the event giving rise to the claim.

10.3 Section 10.1 and Section 10.2 do not limit either Party's liability for its breach
of Section 8, for fraud, or for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10.4 The Parties agree that the allocation of risk in this Section 10 is a material part
of the bargain and is reflected in the fees.

11. MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION

11.1 Vendor shall defend Customer against third-party claims that the Platform infringes
a United States patent, copyright, or trade secret, and shall pay settlements it approves
and damages finally awarded on such claims.

11.2 Customer shall defend Vendor against third-party claims arising from Customer Data
that Customer submitted in breach of this Agreement, and shall pay settlements it
approves and damages finally awarded on such claims.

11.3 Each indemnity in this Section 11 is conditioned on the indemnified Party giving
prompt written notice of the claim, permitting the indemnifying Party to control the
defense, and cooperating at the indemnifying Party's reasonable expense.

11.4 Neither Party is obligated to indemnify the other for claims caused by the
indemnified Party's own negligence or misconduct.

12. TERMINATION AND SUSPENSION

12.1 Vendor may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, effective
immediately upon written notice, without any liability to Customer beyond a refund of
prepaid subscription fees covering the unused portion of the Term.

12.2 Vendor may also, at its sole discretion, terminate this Agreement or suspend
Customer's access to the Platform if Vendor discontinues the Platform, reallocates
capacity, or determines that continuing to serve Customer is no longer consistent with
Vendor's business plans. Vendor is not required to state a reason for any action taken
under this Section 12.

12.3 Customer shall have no right to terminate this Agreement before the end of the Term
except in the case of Vendor's material breach that remains uncured for sixty (60) days
after Customer's written notice describing the breach. Customer remains obligated to pay
the full subscription fees for the entire Term notwithstanding any early termination by
Vendor under Section 12.1 or Section 12.2, other than the refund described in Section
12.1.

12.4 Upon expiration or termination, Customer's access to the Platform ends, Customer may
export Customer Data as provided in Section 5.3, and Sections 5, 8, 10, 11, and 14
survive.

13. INSURANCE

13.1 Each Party shall maintain the insurance coverage customary for its business,
including commercial general liability coverage of at least one million dollars
($1,000,000) per occurrence, and shall provide a certificate of insurance on reasonable
request.

14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, excluding its
conflict of laws rules.

14.2 Before starting litigation, the Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through
good-faith discussions between senior representatives for at least thirty (30) days after
written notice of the dispute.

14.3 Any litigation shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Cook
County, Illinois, and each Party consents to venue there.

15. GENERAL

15.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written
consent, except to a successor by merger or by purchase of substantially all of its
assets, provided the successor assumes this Agreement in writing.

15.2 Neither Party is liable for a failure or delay in performance caused by an event
beyond its reasonable control, provided the affected Party notifies the other promptly
and resumes performance as soon as it reasonably can.

15.3 The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create a
partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

15.4 Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight
courier, or by certified mail to the addresses stated above, and are effective on
receipt.

15.5 A waiver of any provision is effective only if in writing and signed by the waiving
Party, and does not waive any other provision or any later breach.

15.6 This Agreement, including its Schedules, is the complete statement of the Parties'
agreement on this subject and supersedes all prior proposals. It may be amended only by a
written document signed by both Parties.

15.7 If a court finds any provision unenforceable, that provision shall be limited to the
minimum extent necessary so that the remainder of this Agreement continues in effect.
